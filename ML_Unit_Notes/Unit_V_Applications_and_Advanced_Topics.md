# Unit V – Applications and Advanced Topics

## 1. SVM (Support Vector Machine)

SVM ek powerful supervised learning algorithm hai jo classification aur regression dono ke liye use hota hai. Ye maximum margin boundary dhoondhta hai jo classes ko best tareeke se separate kare.

### Main Idea

- Best separating hyperplane find karna
- Support vectors important hote hain

### Advantages

- Works well in high-dimensional space
- Useful for small and medium datasets

---

## 2. Bayesian Learning

Bayesian learning probabilistic reasoning par based hoti hai. Ye prior knowledge aur observed data ke basis par posterior probability compute karti hai.

### Important Concepts

- Prior probability
- Likelihood
- Posterior probability

### Usefulness

Ye uncertain situations aur probabilistic inference ke liye useful hai.
